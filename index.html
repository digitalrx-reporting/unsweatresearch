Cetaphil benchmark — what's different

Overlap

Aloe extract, panthenol, citric acid. Both fragrance-free.

Difference

UNSWEAT skips glycerin, using 1,2-hexanediol/methylpropanediol instead; leans on witch hazel, chamomile, centella rather than Cetaphil's niacinamide/vitamin E.

Flag — open as of Aug 4 UNSWEAT's preservative is benzalkonium chloride — effective, but higher irritation potential than Cetaphil's gentler phenoxyethanol/multi-acid system. Flagged directly against the "safe for facial use" / "gentle cleansing" claim the brand wants to make; also missing the makeup/oil-removal ingredients Cetaphil has. Resolution pending with the manufacturer.

Regulatory & labeling constraints

Do Not Flush

Required by CA, OR, IL, NJ, WA, CO for premoistened nonwoven wipes marketed for facial/personal use — symbol + text, ≥2% of principal panel. Federal WIPPES Act passed the Senate, not yet law. Avoid "flushable / sewer-safe / septic-safe" claims.

Copy restrictions

Avoid: "antibacterial," "kills germs," "relieves ___." Use "sanitize / sterilize" instead. Required on-pack: ingredient list, "For External Use – Avoid Contact with Eyes," "Made in China."

Packaging & SKU status

Final design approved 7/28 (clean layout, neon-green/purple-blue palette, wave motif). Plastic-lid color print adds $0.08/unit + ~$5,900 mold fee, so the cover ships as a printed sticker (7-day lead time). Barcode 884400729866 assigned 7/30. Two SKUs in flight: Cooling wipes and Cleaning/facial wipes on separate ingredient specs.

Pricing is inconsistent across sizes At the 20-count size, UNSWEAT's DTC price ($4.00) sits close to the category average ($4.55) — mid-market. Either the multi-pack discount curve needs to steepen to stay mid-market at scale, or the premium jump needs a stated reason (clinical-grade claim, cooling functional benefit) strong enough to justify sitting near Coterie/Healthybaby territory.
